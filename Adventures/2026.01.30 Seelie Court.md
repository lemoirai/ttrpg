# Seelie Court Excursion Report (Adventure 3)

- **Date:** 3rd of Spar, 237 YFHR
- **Report Author:** Naiobe, Planewalker's Guild Factotum
- **Planewalkers:** Herra, Mote, Suspira, Fyrn, 
- **Briefing Location:** Larel Zazu's Bathouse
- **Portal Location:** As above
- **Destination:** Seelie Court, Feywild
- **Equipment Issued:** Standard monitoring bracelets for portal stability tracking.
- **Mission**: Seek out information related to Sigil's situation, find a portal key to aid Guild research of current portal mechanics, and find any resources for the city. 

## **Summary of Excursion**

The party arrived near the Seelie Court of the Feywild during the Summer Festival, with a minor amount of disorientation from the sudden gravity change of the portal directional change. The group met with King Oberon, the Green Lord, who was sufficiently bemused by the players to allow them to participate in the day's festivities. He offered to allow them to participate in his Hunt for a White Hare, Queen Tatiana's Tournament of Skills, as well as visiting a Hedge Maze with some prizes for anyone who survived it.

The party chose the Hunt for the White Hare first, passing right by the festival's variety of games and food stands where they encountered an elderly elven cleric of Correlon named Aethar. While King Oberron made it clear that contestants could "help" each other as much as they wanted, the party encountered no resistance in finding the White Hare. As quick as the Hare was, it was no match for Herra's speed and she quickly captured it with the aid of the party. Fyrn used their druidic abilities to converse with the rabbit, and eventually came to an agreement to help uncurse the creature in exchange for satisfying the conditions of the Hunt (delivering "the Head of the White Hare") without killing it.

The party took the Hare, named Robin, back to Aethar to remove the creature's curse, battling a group of Blink dogs on the journey back to the festival. Once there, Fyrn pulled the rabbit out of her pack and Aethar uncursed it, transforming it back into its original humanoid form. Robin then bought a white, wooden rabbit at one of the stalls in the festival, and assured the party it would satisfy their obligation to Oberon. While not amused, Oberon conceded that the party had technically satisfied the conditions of the Hunt and gave them a portal key, a coiled spiral of bone, that leads somewhere from Sigil to an estate in the Feywild with plenty of farmland that Oberon assured the party was in "wonderful" condition.

Before returning to Sigil, the party spent some more time at the festival and accepted Queen Titania's invitation to participate in the tournament. They found a magic item shop with a talkative raccoon, named Figgy Bidness, that "sold" magic items that bestowed benign curses on the party members that accepted items from it. Most of the party walked away with at least one item and eventually had Aethar remove the curses, while Suspira declined to pay to remove her curse that makes blood smell like something different every day.

At Queen Titania's, each of the party members accepted one of five challenges: Herra: With Skill, Fyrn: With Wit, Suspira: With Wage, Mote: With Truth, and Aestra: With Out. Herra convinced a phantom of her mother that she had not been misbehaving. Fyrn was unsuccessful in amusing a tiny audience with her ability to summon a swarm of rats. Mote lost a memory to a mirror, though no one knows what it was. Suspira impressed Titania by possessing the willpower to wear the tackiest outfit she could put together. Aestra fought herself, literally, and won. Titania awarded the party a Wand of the War Mage for their efforts.

The party decided against participating in the Hedge Maze, and returned before their luck with the fey turned against them.


## **Important Findings**

- **Portal Key to Feywild Estate** - While Oberon was not forthcoming with the exact location of the portal the key opens, he did confirm that it is located in Sigil and the estate contains a significant amount of farmland. Our initial research indicates that the Sigil side of the portal may be located somewhere on the Wildside of the city.
