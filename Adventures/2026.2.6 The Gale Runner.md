# Gale Runner Rescue Report (Adventure 4)

- **Date:** 7th of Spar, 237 YFHR
- **Report Author:** Naiobe, Planewalker's Guild Factotum
- **Planewalkers:** Suspira, Fyrn, Aestra, Lash, Herra, Aundria
- **Briefing Location:** Lower Ward
- **Portal Location:* Vane 17, Lower Ward
- **Destination:** Gale Runner Ship, Shadowfell 
- **Equipment Issued:** Standard monitoring bracelets for portal stability tracking.
- **Mission**: Find out what happened to the Gale Runner and its captain, render aid, and find the shipment of parts they were hauling that can repair Sigil's air purification system.

## **Summary of Excursion**

I took the party to the portal we identified, using the trade records recovered from Lunia and Orthys, as a potential path to the Gale Runner. After I briefed them on the importance of recovering the parts aboard to successfully repair Sigil's air filtration systems, we arrived to find Mathis and Berowyn struggling to hold the portal open. While we managed to keep the connection open long enough for the party to enter and conduct their business, the portal was almost entirely closed for most of their trip.

The party arrived in the cargo hold of the Gale Runner, where they found the elemental parts delivery for Sigil and a black spike driven into the door to the rest of the ship. They also encountered a mephit that explained it had been kidnapped and was looking for a way home before skittering back into the vent it had come out of. Utilizing some tools from the cargohold, they removed the spike, which the ship seemingly reacted to, and then proceeded into the ship.

Inside the ship they encountered additional, less friendly mephits, several crew members of the ship who turned hostile immediately, and additional black spikes. The party defended themselves against the crew and continued removing black spikes which increasingly freed Robyn who had become trapped inside the ship by the dark devices. The last spike was on the top deck, where the party encountered and battled with the final crew members who attacked the party with a pair of ballista. They were quickly defeated with Robyn's aid, and the crew's leader trapped inside the bridge. The party captured him and removed the Dimensional Shackles from the helm, the final step to fully freeing Robyn from being trapped in the ship.

Through their interrogations of the crew, the party confirmed that the ship was indeed located in the Shadowfell and that the crew had mutinied and turned on Robyn, intending to turn it over to a nearby ship of Vecna worshippers. While Robyn was anxious to go after the ship, worrying that the Vecnans may have already claimed other Djinn ships like theirs, the party opted to retreat to the Robyn's home in the Elemental Plane of Air so they could repair their ship instead of rushing into a battle without reinforcements. For their aid, Robyn gave the party the parts Sigil needed, a crate of fine wine valued at 2000 gold pieces, and a whistle that can act as a portal key to return to Robyn's ship.

## **Important Findings**

- **The Vecnan Fleet** - Vecna worshippers are capturing Djinn operated ships and performing some kind of dark ritual to take control of the ship. At least one such ship is somewhere in the Shadowfell.

- **Dimensional Shackles & Black Spike** - Two of the items used to imprison Robyn in their ship, Robyn believes these items may be helpful to the Guild's investigations in the current portal situation.

- **Whistle Portal Key** - When used near a doorway, archway or other common portal location in Sigil, the whistle can open a portal to Robyn's ship the Gale Runner.

- ***Crack Portal***** Challenges** - The guild believes the portal was initially harder to open and keep open due to the destination being in the Shadowfell. It was easier to reopen it and keep it open after the Gale Runner left that plane.
