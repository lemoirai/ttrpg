# Othrys Excursion Report (Adventure 2)

- **Date:** 2nd of Spar, 237 YFHR
- **Report Author:** Naiobe, Planewalker's Guild Factotum
- **Planewalkers:** Lash, Herra, Mote, Saga, Gwyneth
- **Portal Location:** Basement of the abandoned food warehouse near the Greasy Pit.
- **Destination:** Othrys, 1st layer of Carceri
- **Equipment Issued:** Standard monitoring bracelets for portal stability tracking, Wand of Tenser's Floating Disc.
- **Mission**: Seek out information related to Sigil's situation and if possible, find potential sources of food for the city before famine takes hold of the city.

## **Summary of Excursion**

The party and I met outside the warehouse containing the Orthys portal where I briefed them on the need to find potential food sources for Sigil before leading them to the portal in the basement. Upon arrival to the basement, we discovered an Incantifier had already cracked the party and sent their own group through. He attempted to attack us but I managed to counter his spell and the party subdued him while I took over the ritual keeping the portal open. I gave the party instructions and the wand and they jumped through the portal.

Upon arrival, the party found themselves at the entrance of a farm in the middle of a bog in Othrys, first layer of Carceri. There they met with a local named Tristan who informed them that the Incantifiers had come through and incinerated a group of guards at the farm. They persuaded him to give them a safe shortcut to the farm to save time. At the farm, the party discovered a group of workers, likely petitioners of Carceri, working the tuber farm knee deep in the waters of the River of Styx. After convincing some of the guards to leave, the party dispatched the rest, securing the outside of the farm including a **silvered**** scimitar **found on the guard captain.  

They proceeded into the main Overseer's building where they found three more Incantifiers tossing the building and rifling through some documents in a floor safe. Two of the Incantifiers were killed including their leader, while the third was captured and interrogated by the party. The **documents **they found revealed that the farm had previously been used as a work camp by the Fated for the most egregious tax evaders before the Faction War, and the camp must have been reclaimed by the Gehreleths after the portal to Sigil closed. The **Incantifier's orders** and interrogation revealed that they stole the guild's Crack Portal spell, they wanted to get to this portal before we did, they knew the work camp had previously belonged to the Fated somehow, that they were specifically there to look for that sword, and that they were instructed not to use it under any circumstances.

The party released the captured Incantifier, secured seven crates of the tuber flour with the help of the farm workers, drained the farm of the Styxian waters, and made their way back to the portal. It is recommended that we return to check on the welfare of the workers once we find a way to reopen the same portal again. At the portal the party encountered **Brixtoltik**, the Gehreleth overseer of the farm who attacked them alongside Tristan, the previously helpful guide. The party dispatched them both, and then returned to Sigil with seven crates of the tuber flour. The bodies of the Incantifiers were expelled from the portal when it closed, including the one they had taken captive who had apparently been killed by locals after they freed him.


## **Important Findings**

- **Incantifier Orders** - These revealed that the Incantifiers stole the guild's Crack Portal spell from the Guild, they wanted to get to this portal before we did, they knew the work camp had previously belonged to the Fated somehow, that they were specifically there to look for that sword, and that they were instructed not to use it under any circumstances.

- **Fated Overseer Documents** - Just like with the Lunia Monastery records, many of the documents contain details about trade routes through Sigil to other locations in the Outer Planes. Scouts are already out in the city confirming these leads.

- **The Unidentified Scimitar** - The party recovered the magical scimitar off of the body of the guard captain of the farm. It has resisted my attempts to identify its properties, though we know for sure it is silvered and holds a basic enchantment.

- **Crates of Tuber Flour** - While it smells disgusting and needs purification by a cleric to be safe to eat, the waters of the River of Styx empowers the flour to be highly nutrient rich. A tea-bag of flour is enough to feed a person for a day. The party secured enough to feed the entire Hive Ward for a week.
