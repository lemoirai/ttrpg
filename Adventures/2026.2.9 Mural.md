#  Mural Portal Report (Adventure 5)

- **Date:** 9th of Spar
- **Report Author:** Naiobe, Planewalker's Guild Factotum
- **Planewalkers:** Lash, Herra, Mote, Gwyneth
- **Briefing Location:** Market Ward, Courtyard of Mysterious Mural
- **Portal Location: ** Front door of a store in the Mural
- **Destination:** Alluvius Ruskin's Maze
- **Equipment Issued:** Standard monitoring bracelets for portal stability tracking.

## **Summary of Excursion**

I have seen the Lady of Pain. Neither she nor her Dabus attendants said anything to us, but I can say for certain that she is unequivocally, as alive and active in the city as ever. My hands are still shaking as I write this report. The excursion started relatively normally. I gave them their briefing, making note of the oddity that there is a portal that's supposed to go somewhere in Sigil that was just as shut as all the ones that leave the city. A few hours after I cracked the portal and the party headed through, my guards and I were attacked by a group of bloods that we now know are tied to the Doomguard & Xaositects. While the watchmen died bravely defending me, it was only the unexpected intervention of a group of cutters in dark green masks that kept the first group from ending me entirely. As soon as the threat was gone, they left without a word.

On the other side of the portal, the party arrived to an eerie mirror of the Market Ward covered in fog and gray light. A pair of touts greeted them and vied for the right to guide the party through what they indicated was one of the Lady's mazes. After seeing through the disguise of Djhek’nlarr, an illegal mapmaker and githyanki posing as a githzerai, the party went with Kylie, a young tiefling woman who offered to help them in exchange for a magic item. While Kylie was clearly an opportunist with a price, she apparently dealt with the party fairly once they had paid it.

Kylie took them to the camp of Trolan the Mad, a bard with a group of followers and who they found singing an illegal song he wrote about the Lady of Pain that I've instructed them all never to repeat. At Trolan's request, Lash put on a performance for his followers that gave him inspiration for new songs to write and as a thank you, he gave them a Lantern of Revealing which he said may come in handy in the maze (though the party wound up not needing it).

Kylie guided the party to Tivvum's Antiquities, a portal key shop tended by an elderly tiefling wizard named Alluvius "Lu" Ruskin. She offered to trade them all the portal keys she had to spare in exchange for the scimitar Gwyneth carried that they had recovered from the tuber farm on Orthys. During negotiations, she revealed that the scimitar likely had a dormant but sentient persona carried in the soul gem in the hilt, though she was not specific about who it was. She attacked the party when they declined to accept her deal. Between her magic and the golems made of parts from her shop, she put up a significant fight with the party, but they managed to put down her golems and knock old Lu out. Kylie helped them ransack the shop of keys and Lu's notes about the keys in exchange for the Lantern of Revealing, and they recovered several mostly drained, but still useful magic items but from Lu's body.

The party left Lu's shop and return to Trolan's camp, where several of his followers (Boariss, Bahrak, and Timmee) had decided they wished to abandon their worship of the Lady of Pain with Trolan to return to Sigil with the party. They were ambushed in the streets by Djhek’nlarr and a pair of skeletal minotaurs, who they dispatched with such ease that her Shadow Demon patron Ly'Kritch opted not to engage the party alongside Djhek’nlarr. The party then returned to Sigil with Kylie, and the others where they found me amongst a pile of corpses.

The Lady of Pain arrived almost immediately after they arrived. The group that escaped the mazes with the party immediately fell to their knees and began apologizing while I stood there mouth agape waiting for her to kill us all. But instead, two of her Dabus simply dismantled the mural that once held the portal to Lu's maze, and then the three of them floated away. Kylie disappeared shortly thereafter, but the other three have offered their services to the guild.


## **Important Findings**

- Thirteen portals keys and a journal that describes where to find all of their portals, how to open them, and where each of them go.

- Alluvius Ruskin had a battle with Rowan Darkwood of the Fated that nearly leveled her shop during the Faction War. She was mazed shortly thereafter for attempting to steal the Lady of Pain's power.

- Gwyneth's scimitar holds some kind of sentient entity in the gemstone of the hilt. Though it is currently dormant, it held great interest to Lu.

- The party recovered six shadow gems from Lu's shop that contain the souls of six of Lu & Ly'Kritch's victims. Mote claimed these on behalf of the Raven Queen, and will see to it that the souls are released and sent on their way to their appropriate afterlife.
